3. cooling drag and cylinder-head-temperature operating margin;

## 4. Runway-independent system: tactical cause of R3 marginal value

**A good float fighter by itself is NOT a resilient air defence network.** For a forward bay/lagoon with a 4–8 airplane detachment, every site must actually have:
- sheltered taxi / takeoff lane surveyed for tide, current, wave and debris;
- dispersal moorings and camouflage **separate from runway airfield**;
- trained tenders and mechanic boat, cranes/rails, salvage, fire-fighting, pilot pickup, usable spare props and corrosion kits;
- independent fuel drums/tanks, ammunition and auxiliary electric generation/radio, early-warning watch/coastal scout; communications to fleet/land fighter interception command;
- secure access against enemy coast raiders/RAF/USN strafing, and protected / alternate bays;
- a sortie roster (already airborne, hot alert moored, maintenance, unavailable), NOT "six aircraft = six on station".

**Temporal use (no invented historical scramble time):**
- **first strike:** enemy may hit surprise; parked/moored float fighters can be shot up; US Yorktown first Tulagi blow remains successful in [14];
- **after first report:** if the sheltered inland lagoon and watch survived, **2–5** fighters from a small detachment can fly or begin alert intercept, escort salvagers and deny unopposed repeated waves, conditional on readiness, weather and transit;
- **second strike / next daylight:** coordinate with carrier/land Zero fighters to enlarge a short local interception. MO [14]'s **4–5 floats + 10–12 carrier Zeros** exemplifies complementary support; float detachment does not by itself explain every US loss;
- **after runway destroyed:** first re-establish ISR/contact relay, then patch fighter response, convoy escort/shore defence; an enemy can still attack separate float bases, wreck fuel boats or suppress cranes. Seaplanes and tender facilities need tailored AA and ASW protection.

**Operational value functions:** maintain contact, shield flying boats and road/port construction boats, deny unescorted photo/scout aircraft, supplement CAP at a vulnerable time, keep a reconnaissance relay when a land runway is out of action, and reduce how many major fleet fighters must patrol every bay. These are real tactical insurance services **without** seeking 24-hour high-altitude parity versus RAF Spitfire Vc/VIII or late US carrier fighters.
