## 1. 系列全体

| 社内型式 | 世界内名 | 構成 | 排気量 | 主用途 |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| O2 | 暁一一型 | 130×150mm ×14 | 27.87L | 1935–37主力 |

## 6. O4 / 昴 — CLOSED product target

research:
- 1933–34 paper study: 130×150×18 = 35.84L
- 1934–35: O1由来9cyl high-load mule、550→650hp
- 1935–36: 18cyl crank/case/torsion/9×2 cooling rig
- late 1936: O4-0 first complete、1000→1100→1200
- 1937 H1: complete research engines 2–3、87oct 1250–1300、92oct 1350–1400
- 1937 autumn: 海軍次期戦闘機要求がformal productization trigger
- 1938 Q1 product prototype、Q2 1400–1450 + two-speed、summer first 1500
- 1938 H2: 150h endurance / flight
- 1939 Q1 qualification、H1 low-rate

## 7. O5 / 峰 — PROVISIONAL

岳18。145×160×18 = 47.56L。

岳がlarge-bore cylinder / thermal / manufacturing、昴が9×2 mechanics / torsion / oil / cooling / reductionを供給する。

roadmap:
- 1933–35 paper/research
- 1936 large 9-cylinder mechanical rig
- 1937 H2 preliminary full study
- 1938 H1 firing mule / crank / case
- 1938 H2 full engine construction
- 1939 H1 O5-0 first run 1400→1600
- 1939 H2 1600–1750
- 1940 candidate qualification 1750–1850
- 1941 1900–2000
- later 2000–2200+

initial target:
- one-stage two-speed
- 92oct
- dry 880–930kg
- dia 1.34–1.38m
- takeoff 1800hp
- 30min 1650–1700
- continuous 1450–1550
- TBO initial 150–200h、mature 250–300h

1800/2700 BMEP約12.7bar、2000/2800約13.4bar。

## 8. 役割分担

- **暁14 27.87L:** healthy 900–1150hp、attack upper 1250。軽戦・司偵・高速双発。
- **昴18 35.84L:** healthy 1400–1650、upper 1700。高速単発・双発。
- **岳14 36.99L:** healthy 1200–1450、upper 1500。爆撃機・大型機・長時間。
- **峰18 47.56L:** healthy 1750–2100、upper 2200+。将来大型高出力。

昴と岳は排気量が近いが、気筒数・径・回転・用途が違うため併存する。
