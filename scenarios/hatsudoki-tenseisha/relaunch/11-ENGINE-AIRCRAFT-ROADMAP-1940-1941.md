### O3 / 岳

- commercial product remains the reliable 145×160mm ×14 large-cylinder family
- initial/mature products around 1320–1450PS, with two-speed growth
- 岳 commercial rating and high-load research are separate clocks
- 1550–1650PS-class research is allowed as O5 cylinder/thermal support even if not mass-productized

### O5 / 峰 — CLOSED architecture, maturity by date

- 145×160mm ×18 = 47.56L
- core intent: 岳 large-cylinder thermal/manufacturing + 昴 9×2 mechanics/torsion/oil/cooling/reduction
- intended from outset for both high-performance single-engine fighters and large twins
- 1940 candidate: 1800–1850PS class
- 1941 core: about 2000PS / 2800rpm, low 30min 1870–1920PS, high 30min 1750–1820PS @6–6.5km, continuous 1650–1700PS
- dry roughly 915–940kg for the 2000PS class
- initial TBO about 200h with 250h mature target
- product philosophy: not peak-only; unusually long usable high output close to takeoff power
- 1941-12-07: 2000PS-class early-production/qualification branch exists; 2150–2200PS growth remains development, not current field standard

### Exhaust / high altitude / turbo

- rearward low-backpressure exhaust and cooling-outlet integration are mature design methods
- ejector exhaust + cooling-flow entrainment is the preferred practical exhaust-energy route
- turbo research **exists**, but is not the main product line
- scarce heat-resistant alloys and precision capacity are preferentially spent on valves, hot-section engine parts, supercharger/reduction reliability, and ejector systems
- high-altitude product path first favors two-stage mechanical supercharging + charge cooling
- a major Ni/Cr resource change (e.g. successful New Caledonia exploitation) may justify a much thicker turbo program, but materials alone do not solve turbine life, balance, bearings, seals, controls, or production yield

### Engine control

By 1941 the Owari direction is staged semi-automation:
- boost limiter / overspeed governor / mixture schedule / oil thermostat
- throttle–prop–mixture coordination
- supercharger shift indication/interlock
- modular manual fallback
Future O6-class engines are expected to use near-single-power-lever mechanical/hydraulic coordination, but that is not yet a universal 1941 field standard.

### Army next main / general fighter — CLOSED lineage, PROVISIONAL 1941 configuration

**Why there is no mass-production “O4 Ki-43” branch**
- 1937–39の陸軍は、海軍の九六式三号のような「暁搭載高速戦闘機を大きな部隊規模で先行運用した厚い足場」を持たず、まず成熟したO2C / 暁二一で長距離・野戦・量産主力を早く成立させる合理性が高かった。
- Ki-43 / 九九戦は実際に約550km/h級、長航続、低い翼面荷重、野戦整備性を早期に成立させ、1939–40の主力として十分成功した。
- したがって成立済みKi-43へ重いO4を押し込み、前胴・prop・CG・脚・翼・燃料系まで再設計して「同じKi-43」として量産する枝は採らない。
- これは **O4 / 昴が陸軍主力戦闘機に不要という判断ではない**。O4能力を先に支払う別枝としてKi-44を使い、その実測を次期一般戦闘機へ戻す。

**1940 strategic trigger**
- 1940年にはO4 Ki-44が600km/h超級の高速・上昇・急降下構造を実機で示し、海軍側でもO4系艦戦の性能が見え始める。
- 欧州・米英の新型戦闘機公表値 / 戦闘報告も、600km/h級・重武装・高速降下を「特殊迎撃機だけの領域」ではなく次世代主力の基準へ押し上げる。
- よって陸軍は **Ki-43の航続・野戦性を保ちつつ、Ki-44のO4高速性・高速操縦・重武装余地・強い脚を統合する次期一般戦闘機** を1940年から具体化する。

**competing branches — 1940**
1. **Ki-43 O4 re-engine comparison:** 技術比較は行うが、必要変更が大きく、量産Ki-43の美点と共通性を失うため本命化しない。
2. **Ki-44 general-fighter development:** 翼面積・燃料・combat flap・視界・野戦整備性・防御を増し、Ki-44の短航続 / 高着陸速度寄り性格を一般戦闘機側へ戻す。**central parent branch**。
3. **clean-sheet / O5 growth study:** O5 / 峰の成熟を見越すが、O5完成待ちでO4機の開発を止めない。

**project identity**
- 出発点はKi-44の長距離 / 一般戦闘型でよい。
- ただし翼、燃料系、脚取付、尾翼、胴体容積、防弾、武装配置を順次変更すると、既存Ki-44の派生として管理する意味が薄くなる。
- **1941中に「Ki-44改」から独立した次期主力projectへ別機化する**のをcentralとする。
- 史実Ki-84に近い役割・系譜を持つため分析上は **Ki-84-equivalent** と呼べるが、世界内の正式Ki番号 / 制式名は採用時までOPEN。

**development clock — central**
- **1940 Q2:** Ki-43後継 / 次期一般戦闘機performance study開始。
- **1940 Q3:** Ki-43 O4比較とKi-44万能化案をwind-tunnel / layout / load study。後者を主案化。
- **1940 Q4–1941 Q1:** 拡大翼・増燃料・防御余地を持つ「Ki-44一般戦闘型」試作機を設計 / 製作。

- **1941 Q2–Q3:** 翼・燃料・尾翼・脚・高速操縦・整備性の改修で設計変更量が増え、独立projectへ移行。

## 3. Owari technology diffusion boundary

Do not model every Japanese aircraft as an Owari derivative.

Direct Owari impact is counted only when there is a concrete path:
- comparison installation
- interface package
- military acceptance specification
- joint test
- licensed/secret-patent implementation
- shared supplier/process documentation

Rearward exhaust itself is not a permanent secret. The Owari advantage is system-level measurement, back-pressure/CHT/cooling mapping, durability, process control and integration.

Non-Owari engines such as Kasei, Sakae, Homare may improve from industry-wide diffusion without becoming Owari products.

## 4. Forward research roadmap — NOT current fielded history

These items are preserved so the research teams do not reset, but they are not 1941-12-07 operational facts.

### O6
- last major clean-sheet piston core envisioned by the protagonist
- working architecture: 155×160mm ×18 ≈54.34L
- initial product target about 2500PS; mature 2650–2700PS
- designed for sustained usable power rather than one-minute peak
- likely users: future fast twin/heavy attack aircraft, future interceptor, large aircraft
- not yet a fielded engine

### High-speed machinery / contra-rotation
- common research on high-power reduction gear, remote reduction, large propellers and contra-rotating modules
- contra-rotation is not a universal standard; especially attractive for very-high-power pusher/interceptor layouts
- production adoption remains dependent on endurance, control, maintenance and mission value

### Turbo / gas turbine
- turbocharger research continues at limited scale
- gas-turbine basic team maintains compressor, combustor, turbine-material, high-speed-bearing and balancing research
- protagonist knows the direction of future propulsion but is not a free jet-engine designer

### High-altitude crew systems
Non-Owari but accelerated by stronger high-altitude aircraft:
- reliable demand oxygen and mission-duration oxygen accounting
- heating / demist / defrost
- partial pressurization research
- pressure cockpit + oxygen backup
- anti-G clothing and centrifuge/physiology work can advance earlier because high-speed/high-G operations become routine earlier

### Shinden / Senden / Ren-zan
- architectural studies discussed are future branches only
- pusher/contra, turbo/non-turbo and O6 choices must be re-evaluated against the actual later war situation
- do not treat their explored performance numbers as present-day actor knowledge or guaranteed future outcomes
