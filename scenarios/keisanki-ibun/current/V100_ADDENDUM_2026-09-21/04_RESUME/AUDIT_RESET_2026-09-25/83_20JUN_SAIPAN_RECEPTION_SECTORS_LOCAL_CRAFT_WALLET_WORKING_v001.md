## 11. What this implies for the thick branch

A ~2,000–3,500-man fast reinforcement is physically compatible with the local receiving system if:

1. incoming transport hulls carry the troops / heavy cargo;
2. Saipan retains ~22–30 MR large craft class;
3. H1 + R3-E can both be activated;
4. surface / air / shore cover holds the corridor for roughly **2–3 craft cycles**;
5. the inland routes remain open enough to clear the beach / ramp areas.

The local-boat system is therefore **not the obvious bottleneck** at brigade-slice scale.

The harder bottlenecks become:
- incoming lift hulls;
- U.S. radar / surface interdiction;
- dusk / night air cover;
- duration of temporary local sea control;
- inland absorption under artillery fire.
