# 0. Historical fixed heavy-gun board

Official U.S. campaign histories give the following main Tinian coastal / dual-purpose layout:

## Ushi Point
- **3 x 140-mm coast-defense guns**
- **3 x 120-mm dual-purpose guns**
- additional 75-mm AA / automatic weapons

## Faibus San Hilo Point
- **3 x 140-mm coast-defense guns**
- local lighter weapons / nearby field artillery

## Asiga Bay
- **4 x 140-mm coast-defense guns**
- **3 x 76.2-mm dual-purpose guns**
- local lighter weapons

## Gurguan Point / airfield
- **3 x 120-mm dual-purpose guns**
- 25-mm AA concentration

## Tinian Harbor / Tinian Town
- **4 x 120-mm dual-purpose guns**
- **3 x 6-inch naval/coast-defense guns**
- additional lighter weapons

Total historical heavy naval layer:
- **10 x 140-mm CD**
- **10 x 120-mm DP**
- **3 x 6-inch CD**
plus the Army mobile-artillery system.

Army mobile reserve included:
- **12 x Type 94 75-mm mountain guns** as a central mobile-artillery contingent,
plus local infantry-gun / lighter artillery.

Do not treat every fixed gun as having unrestricted 360-degree land-fire arcs.

# 3. Reach classification by battery

## Ushi Point 140-mm — HARD CROSS-CHANNEL CORE
Range:
**YES to all major southern-Saipan boxes.**

Can physically reach:
- Agingan;
- Obyan;
- Aslito;
- Nafutan;
- large portions of Saipan Channel.

This is the single cleanest fixed heavy-battery contributor to the Saipan campaign.

Branch value:
- precomputed Aslito / southern-road / support-water target cards;
- multiple OPs;
- short firing pulses;
- protected reactivation.

---

## Ushi Point 120-mm — HARD CROSS-CHANNEL CORE
Range:
**YES.**

~16 km range comfortably covers:
- Agingan;
- Obyan;
- Aslito;
- Nafutan.

This battery is especially important because:
- high practical rate of fire;
- dual-purpose role;
- fixed target geometry.

Tradeoff:
AA defense and ground/sea fire compete for the same guns / crews / ammunition / exposure.

---

## Faibus San Hilo 140-mm — RANGE YES / ARC OPEN
Range:
- Agingan: comfortable;
- Aslito: inside envelope;
- Nafutan: near-middle / upper useful envelope.

Historical location proves strong northwest/coastal fields of fire.
Exact ability to traverse toward each Saipan land box requires emplacement-arc closure.

Even if land arcs are restricted, this battery is highly relevant to:
- western Saipan Channel;
- U.S. ships supporting southern Saipan;
- suppression ships approaching Tinian.

Branch:
high-value for alternate OP / target-board preparation.

---

## Asiga Bay 140-mm — RANGE YES / ARC & MASKING OPEN
Pure range:
**YES to southern Saipan.**

Geography:
- Agingan ~7.7 km;

But historical mission orientation was eastern-coast defense.
Therefore do not automatically credit all four guns against Saipan.

If even a subset can bear north/northwest:

## Tinian Harbor/Town 120-mm — MAINLY SEA / LOCAL DEFENSE
Saipan land targets are generally beyond ~16 km.

- not a direct Aslito gun.

This distinction prevents over-credit.

# 4. Mobile Type-94 75-mm battery family

Historical mobile pool:
**12 guns.**

Range:
~8 km class.

From historical central/southern reserve positions:
Saipan land targets are mostly out of range.

But Branch candidate-position preparation changes this.

If selected 75-mm sections/batteries occupy pre-surveyed **northern Tinian C2/C3 sites** near:
- Ushi;
- Asiga;
- northwestern plateau / Faibus-side network,

then they can reach:
- Agingan-side southern coast;
- Obyan / closest southern-Saipan boxes;
- landing/support-water areas;

but generally **not deep Aslito** unless a longer-range weapon is substituted.

Thus:
> computation + prepared northern positions converts part of the mobile 75-mm pool into a genuine cross-channel light-artillery option.

This should remain a bounded allocation.
Do not strip the entire mobile counterattack artillery reserve merely to fire across the channel.

# 5. Branch candidate-position effect

The large Branch gain is not to move fixed 140/120-mm guns.

It is to create a larger family of:

- mobile 75-mm positions;
- OPs;
- plotting / liaison sites;
- communication relays;
- ammunition first-load caches;
- dummy positions;
- alternate observation geometry.

For the fixed heavy guns, precomputation prepares:
- Saipan fixed-reference firing cards;
- channel sectors;
- likely ship-support boxes;
- Aslito / road / base target boxes where range/arc permits;
- transfer between alternate OPs.

For the mobile guns, it also prepares:
- the actual future firing position.

# 7. U.S. suppression consequence

Historical U.S. behavior gives a hard scale.

From 20 Jun through 9 Jul, while Saipan fighting was still underway:
- XXIV Corps Artillery fired **331 missions / 7,571 rounds** on Tinian;
- this was roughly **one fifth** of its Saipan-period artillery expenditure;
- observation aircraft flew daily over Tinian for registration and intelligence.

Therefore:
> **Tinian already consumed a major U.S. fire-support wallet historically.**

Branch Tinian has:
- better protection;
- better prepared OPs;
- more physically prepared alternate/mobile sites;
- more precomputed target data.

So the same U.S. expenditure is less likely to produce permanent neutralization.

This does NOT mean simply add another 20% U.S. artillery deficit.
It means:
- a historically large suppression bill already existed;
- Branch raises the number of repeat missions needed to maintain the same degree of silence.

# 8. Saipan campaign tactical consequences

## 15 Jun
Primary Tinian contributions:
- Ushi/Faibus heavy guns vs fire-support ships / channel;
- Ushi 120/140 cross-channel fire;
- U.S. ships/air diverted to Tinian suppression.

No bridgehead-collapse threshold crossed automatically.

## 16–19 Jun
Direct land-fire core:
- Ushi;
- conditional Asiga/Faibus sectors.

Targets:
- Agingan / Obyan routes;
- southern artillery/support;
- Aslito functional boxes if arc permits.

Main effect:
- less concentrated U.S. suppression against Saipan;
- more Aslito work interruption.

## 20–26 Jun
U.S. 155-mm counterbattery from Saipan becomes a major opposing system.

Japanese best practice:
- short synchronized fire windows;
- do not duel continuously;
- fire during:
  - relief approach;
  - U.S. ship maneuver;
  - Saipan local counterattack;
  - local aviation/observation windows.

## late Jun–5/6 Jul
Northern Tinian heavy batteries remain a potential external fire axis even as Tapotchau degrades.

This supports:
- Aslito harassment;
- southern support disruption;
- broader observation.

Not continuous base denial.

# 9. Main technical finding

The geographical audit rejects two extremes.

Wrong:
> all Tinian heavy guns can pound Aslito.

Also wrong:
> Tinian fire is only local anti-landing defense.

Correct:
> **a northern Tinian heavy-gun core has genuine range to southern Saipan and Aslito, while southern/western batteries mainly impose an anti-ship / suppression tax in the channel and around Tinian.**

That division makes the Branch artillery network stronger without inventing impossible fire arcs.
