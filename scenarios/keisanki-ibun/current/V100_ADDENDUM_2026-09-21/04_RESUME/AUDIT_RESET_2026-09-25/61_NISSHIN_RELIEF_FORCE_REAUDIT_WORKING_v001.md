## 2. Historical physical anchor

Nisshin is not being given a new ahistorical transport mission.

Historical IJN use in October 1942 demonstrates the mission class directly:
- Nisshin and Chitose were used as fast reinforcement transports to Guadalcanal;

Nisshin is too valuable and too large to sit in the exposed forward daytime anti-surface target box.
