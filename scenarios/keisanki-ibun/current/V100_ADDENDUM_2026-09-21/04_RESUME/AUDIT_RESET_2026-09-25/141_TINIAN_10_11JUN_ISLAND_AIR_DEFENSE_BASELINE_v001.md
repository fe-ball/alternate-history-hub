# 7. Fighter / AA interaction

The Tinian defense is a layered system.

Sequence:

1. radar/visual warning;
2. standing CAP/revector;
3. alert scramble;
4. fighter engagement;
5. attackers forced into less ideal geometry;
6. 120/75-mm engagement where altitude/time permits;
7. 25/20/13-mm terminal fire;
8. dispersal / revetment reduces target destruction;
9. repair / alternate runway converts part of damage into temporary unavailability.

Thus U.S. loss attribution must record:
- fighter;
- heavy/medium AA;
- light AA;
- damaged-return / recovery loss;
- operational abort.

Do not credit a single loss twice.
