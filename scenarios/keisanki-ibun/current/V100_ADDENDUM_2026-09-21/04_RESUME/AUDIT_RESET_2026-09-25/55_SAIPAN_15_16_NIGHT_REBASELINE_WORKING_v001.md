## 2. What changes from the approved model

### Better target selection
Day aerial observation + ground OPs reduce uncertainty around:
- 105-mm support areas;
- high-traffic seam routes;
- vehicle/artillery assembly;
- beach-to-inland logistics lanes.

Night mortar/artillery fires therefore waste fewer missions on stale boxes.

### Better approach selection
Air/photo reports help identify:
- open vs congested routes;
- where U.S. reserves are accumulating;
- where illumination / artillery reaction is strongest.

This improves infiltration route choice, not stealth immunity.

### U.S. is also better prepared
Because Japanese observed fire was more effective during the day, U.S. commanders:
- expect continued fire;
- strengthen local security;
- pre-plan defensive artillery/illumination;
- keep more observation/air liaison attention on the seam.

Thus Japanese advantage does not compound without limit.
