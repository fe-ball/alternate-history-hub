# 0. Historical correction to the release model

Do not hold every APA/AKA at Saipan until T-END.

Historically:
- attack transports/cargo ships departed Saipan after unloading while fighting continued;
- casualties were backloaded;
- some returned to Hawaii and loaded 77th Division personnel/equipment for Guam by early July.

Therefore Branch rule:

> **Large transport turnaround begins as soon as each ship's Saipan task is complete.**
>
> **The assets most strongly trapped until late are landing craft/LVTs, local shore-control systems, NGFS, CVE/direct-support, and their escorts.**

File123's post-T-END release clock applies mainly to the residual forward layer, not to every transport hull.
