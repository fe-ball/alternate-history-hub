# 0. Hard historical correction

A Japanese rocket weapon was physically present on historical Saipan.

U.S. War Department Intelligence Bulletin Vol. III No. 8 (Apr 1945), "Japanese Development of Rocket Weapons", states:

- the first Japanese rocket weapon encountered by U.S. troops was a **Model 10 rocket launcher captured on Saipan**;
- only one launcher of this type was found;
- no instance of its use was reported;
- U.S. intelligence regarded it as a crude early experiment/improvisation.

Weapon:
- wooden/trough launcher;
- separate rocket motor pushes a Navy Type 97 No.6 ~60-kg / 132-lb aerial bomb;
- cited Japanese range:
  - ~770 yd at 30 degrees;
  - ~1,300 yd at 50 degrees;
- accuracy questionable.

Therefore:
> **Japanese rocket technology on Saipan = HARD YES.**
>
> **Historical effective rocket battery on Saipan = NO evidence.**

This distinction matters.
