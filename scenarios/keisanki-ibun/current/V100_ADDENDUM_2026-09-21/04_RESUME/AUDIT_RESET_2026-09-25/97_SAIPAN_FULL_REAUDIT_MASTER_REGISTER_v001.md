# 0. Audit method

For every item below use one or more of the following dispositions.

- **VERIFY** — external/source or repository evidence must be checked.
- **ACCOUNT** — close a finite wallet / population / ammunition / craft / equipment identity.
- **CORRECT** — known model or arithmetic problem; rerun the dependent result.
- **QUALITATIVE** — same tonnage/headcount may produce a different functional state because the right equipment, doctrine, organization or component completeness exists.
- **FREEZE** — potentially decisive change is recognized but must not be propagated until prerequisites are closed.
- **KEEP** — concept is robust and should survive even if exact numbers move.

Hard rule:
> Do not repair the late campaign by pulling it back toward historical dates.  
> Rebuild from the earliest affected checkpoint and allow later dates/results to move.

Hard accounting rule:
> Do not count the same transport tonnage, reinforcement personnel, craft hull, gun, aircraft, engineer plant, ammunition stock, or specialist twice merely because it appears in two functional narratives.

Hard qualitative rule:
> Existing transport-tonnage gains are already paid for elsewhere.  
> The new heavy-equipment audit is principally about **functional completeness and conversion of physical stock into usable systems**, not another free tonnage increase.

# 2. Qualitative differences that must not be lost

These are not new free multipliers. They are existing Branch mechanisms whose consequences must appear when physically relevant.

1. **Functional-completeness logistics**
   - scarce lenses, sights, survey instruments, radios, power supplies, spares and tools arrive with the major equipment they enable.

2. **Distributed optical/observation network**
   - more equipped alternate OPs;
   - less single-point dependence.

3. **Physical stock -> usable system conversion**
   - guns, radars, airfields, craft, vehicles and engineer plant are more likely to have the missing support pieces needed to function.

4. **Reverse-landing / reception planning**
   - pre-surveyed receiving lanes, boat-control, cross-loading, inland routes, alternate points.

5. **Boat mobility**
   - boats are operational maneuver assets, not only logistics hulls.

6. **True counterlanding**
   - local coastal flank/rear insertion is a real option.

7. **Bidirectional logistics**
   - reinforcement in;
   - wounded/specialists out;
   - Tinian lateral movement;
   - craft/crew preservation when appropriate.

8. **Fresh troops as force-regeneration tools**
   - backfill fixed sectors;
   - free experienced local troops;
   - later become usable combat mass after integration.

9. **Selective evacuation with a combat-value gate**
   - preserve future knowledge without prematurely hollowing the final defense.

10. **Fire-data / computation advantage**
    - better preparation, correction, recordkeeping and fixed-box time-fuze employment;
    - no omniscience and no generic accuracy magic.
