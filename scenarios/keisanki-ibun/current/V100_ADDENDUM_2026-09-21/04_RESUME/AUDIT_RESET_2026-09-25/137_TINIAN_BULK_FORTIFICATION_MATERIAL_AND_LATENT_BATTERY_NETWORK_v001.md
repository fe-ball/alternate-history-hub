# 0. Core ruling

Tinian should not be modeled as receiving only residual scraps after Saipan.

The Branch already has:
- Marianas as an A1 defense priority;
- a large March bulk-planning share for the Marianas;
- multiple engineer / fortification battalion-equivalents;
- specialist companies in:
  - airfield;
  - unloading;
  - rock/tunnel;
  - signals/power;
  - roads/bridges;
- better bulk shipping, unloading, survey, planning and plant uptime than historical same-date Marianas.

Saipan remains the principal immediate ground-fortress priority.

But Tinian is simultaneously:
- a major air base;
- a coastal-artillery platform;
- a Saipan flank-support node;
- a transport / reception node;
- an obvious next U.S. objective.

Therefore:
> **Tinian receives a substantial deliberate share of Marianas bulk fortification material, not merely local improvisation.**

Do not assign an exact tonnage here until the March-May island allocation ledger is closed.

# 1. What "substantial bulk material" means physically

Creditable Tinian deliveries include significant quantities of:

- cement / concrete aggregate support where practical;
- reinforcing steel / structural steel;
- timber;
- rails / beams for overhead cover;
- corrugated metal / roofing;
- sandbags / revetment materials;
- barbed wire;
- demolition explosive;
- mines / obstacle material;
- field telephone wire;
- switchboards / batteries;
- generators / power cable;
- pumps / drainage equipment;
- camouflage material;
- construction tools;
- compressors / rock-drilling tools where allocated;
- spare optical / survey equipment;
- ammunition-storage material;
- road / track repair material;
- fuel and lubricants for engineer plant.

Local coral / earth / limestone remain major construction inputs.
Shipping does not need to carry every ton of emplacement mass.

The high-value imported material is what lets local bulk material become a durable fortified system.

# 2. Artillery-specific consequence

For priority batteries and candidate battery sites, the Tinian network can now include more than a paper firing card.

A candidate site may already possess some combination of:

- cut / excavated gun position;
- splinter revetment;
- crew shelter;
- ammunition recess / small magazine;
- covered approach;
- prepared drainage;
- camouflage frame;
- datum / azimuth reference;
- prelaid wire;
- junction box / communications spur;
- prepared OP link;
- access track;
- first-load ammunition plan;
- nearby dummy position.

Thus candidate positions range across:

### C0 — surveyed only
- coordinate / fire card;
- little physical work.

### C1 — survey + access
- route;
- datum;
- minimal clearing.

### C2 — prepared field position
- pit / revetment;
- wire spur;
- local ammunition recess;
- ready for mobile gun occupation.

### C3 — protected alternate
- stronger overhead / crew cover;
- concealed approach;
- better ammo / communication protection;
- reusable after suppression.

### C4 — heavy fixed / cave cell
- only for selected high-priority permanent batteries where terrain / resources justify it.

Do not make every candidate site C3/C4.

But under the Branch bulk-allocation premise:
> **a meaningful family of C2/C3 sites is plausible on Tinian by 15 Jun.**

---

# 3. Why this is stronger than computation alone

Computation alone can prepare:
- coordinates;
- fire cards;
- target grids.

Bulk material + engineer labor converts that into:
- faster occupation;
- crew survival;
- ammunition survival;
- communication survival;
- faster reactivation.

Therefore the Branch advantage is multiplicative in mechanism:

**survey/calculation**
x
**physical candidate-position preparation**
x
**distributed ammunition / signals**
x
**protected observation**

Not a literal numerical multiplier.

# 4. Mobile artillery implication

For 75-mm / other mobile field guns:

Historical-like displacement:
- gun moves;
- finds workable ground;
- emplaces;
- orients;
- reconnects;
- reconstructs useful firing data.

Branch Tinian:
- gun moves to named C2/C3 candidate site;
- route is already known;
- datum exists;
- wire may already exist;
- ammunition recess may already contain a bounded stock;
- fire card exists;
- alternate OP pairing is known.

Therefore:
**post-suppression useful-fire return is much more reliable.**

This is especially powerful during the Saipan campaign because U.S. ground forces cannot physically overrun the candidate-position family.

# 5. Fixed heavy-battery implication

For 120/140/152-mm fixed batteries, bulk material should be credited chiefly to:

- crew shelters;
- ready-ammunition protection;
- larger magazines / subdivision;
- blast/splinter protection;
- protected plotting / telephone rooms;
- alternate OPs;
- cable redundancy;
- camouflage;
- dummy apertures / positions;
- repair stores;
- protected access.

Do not create extra heavy guns merely because material exists.

The gain is:
**the gun remains tactically recoverable after near misses / suppression more often.**

---

# 6. Ammunition depth

A major fortified artillery node is useless without ammunition.

Given:
- deliberate island-defense priority;
- bulk shipping;
- Tinian's role as an air / coast-defense node;

Tinian should not be modeled with only a thin emergency ammunition wallet by default.

Selected qualitative state:
- priority fixed batteries have meaningful sustained stocks;
- ammunition is subdivided rather than concentrated in one magazine;
- mobile artillery has local first-load / short-duration caches at selected alternate sites;
- special ammunition remains weapon-specific and must not be invented.

Do not yet assign exact rounds per gun without an ammunition ledger.

# 7. Effect on U.S. suppression

This materially strengthens the nonlinearity identified in files132-135.

U.S. suppression must now defeat not only:
- one known gun position,

but a system of:
- protected current positions;
- alternate observation;
- prepared mobile-gun sites;
- distributed ammunition;
- prelaid communications;
- dummy positions.

Therefore:
- air/naval reconnaissance must revisit;
- DD/CA/BB counterbattery missions repeat;
- CVE/carrier sorties are spent on suspected positions;
- Saipan 155-mm missions are diverted south;
- some attacks hit prepared-but-empty / dummy sites.

This is primarily a **suppression-efficiency penalty**, not a magical Japanese accuracy increase.

---

# 8. Effect on 15 Jun–9 Jul Saipan campaign

The strongest result is persistence.

### 15 Jun
- Tinian can fire at support shipping / channel geometry;
- protected batteries are harder to silence permanently.

### 16–19 Jun
- U.S. must keep paying a southward suppression bill while also attacking Saipan.

### 20–26 Jun
- Saipan-based 155-mm fire onto Tinian becomes important but not automatically decisive;
- mobile batteries can use prepared C2/C3 alternates;
- Tinian fixed guns can reappear after suppression.

### late Jun–5/6 Jul
- Tinian fire against Aslito / southern support becomes a mature recurring option, not a one-off terminal cameo.

No automatic new casualty total is booked here.

---

# 9. Tinian invasion implication

The same material depth later matters to the Tinian campaign.

The U.S. encounters:
- more protected artillery-support functions;
- more alternate mobile-gun sites;
- more intact local ammunition;
- more false / empty prepared positions;
- more resilient communications.

But Tinian remains:
- flatter than Saipan;
- exposed to enormous Saipan-based artillery;
- vulnerable to isolation.

Therefore a later Tinian replay must derive duration/effect from actual positions and arcs, not from copying Saipan casualty multipliers.

The selected Tinian interpretation is now:

> **not merely surveyed candidate gun positions, but a partially materialized family of prepared artillery positions supported by substantial Marianas bulk construction supply.**

The Branch calculation advantage gives the network its fire-data depth.

The bulk-transport / engineer allocation gives that network:
- earthwork;
- cover;
- wire;
- ammunition protection;
- access;
- survivability.

Together they make Tinian a much more persistent artillery node during the Saipan campaign than the earlier replay credited.
