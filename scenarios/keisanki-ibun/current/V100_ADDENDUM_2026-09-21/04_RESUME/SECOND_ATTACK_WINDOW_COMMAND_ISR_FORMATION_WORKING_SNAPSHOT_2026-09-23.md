# 8. Decision value is enemy behavior, not claimed kills

The key follow-on questions after 一撃 are:

1. **How much damage seems credible?**
2. **What is TF58 actually doing now?**
3. **Is a damaged/separated carrier-class group forming?**
4. **Is the U.S. main body still combat-coherent?**
5. **Is U.S. search pressure now finding the Japanese carriers/surface layer?**
6. **Is a westward U.S. movement beginning?**
7. **How much daylight remains before a surface force could exploit?**
8. **Where are the fast vanguard / heavy support groups at this exact moment?**

A strike report saying “multiple carriers hit” with later reconnaissance showing orderly U.S. formation and active flight decks should be discounted.

A modest hit report followed by:
- 2 carrier-class ships separating east;
- concentrated DD escort;
- rescue/recovery activity;
- persistent main-body reorganization
may imply greater operational damage than the raw hit count.
