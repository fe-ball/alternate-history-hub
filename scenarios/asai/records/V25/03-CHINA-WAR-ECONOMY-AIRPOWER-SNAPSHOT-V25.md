# V25 CHINA WAR / ECONOMY / AIRPOWER SNAPSHOT — 1941-08-11

## 1. Core judgment

China is **not** near disappearance as a state. The war is being sustained by converting hidden forms of capital into time:

- heavy weapons and vehicles;
- radios, optics, medical and maintenance equipment;
- trained technical personnel;
- transport efficiency;
- inventories;
- real wages and currency credibility;
- provincial/rural surplus extracted in cash and kind.

A stable-looking front therefore does not imply stable military quality.

## 2. Military structure

Historical-order baseline entering 1941 remains a very large Nationalist army with millions of personnel and hundreds of nominal divisions, but modern equipment is thin.

V25 interpretation:

- manpower replacement is easier than replacement of artillery, AT/AA, radios, trucks, recovery assets, optics and skilled specialists;
- withdrawal often saves people while abandoning the expensive layer that makes formations capable of modern combined-arms counterattack;
- the result is not automatic unit disappearance but **de-capitalization**.

Do not assign a single national combat-power scalar.

For repeatedly fought-over formations, a **WORKING analytical band** of roughly 20–30% deterioration from January to August in immediately usable heavy-equipment/vehicle capital is permissible for sensitivity analysis where the local battle ledger supports it. This is not a nationwide measured fact and must not be copied to untouched units.

## 3. What remains strong

- large manpower reservoir;
- local defensive fighting;
- mountain/river/poor-road defense;
- dispersion and night movement;
- small-unit counterattack;
- ability to reconstitute nominal formations;
- political ability to continue the war if external aid and central cohesion remain.

## 4. What is weakening

- prolonged corps/army-level offensive mobility;
- artillery density;
- radio-supported command;
- motor transport;
- repair/recovery throughput;
- airfield concentration;
- visible logistics hubs;
- speed of rebuilding after a major campaign.

The direction is toward a growing fraction of the army becoming **light-infantry armies**: difficult to destroy completely, but progressively less able to conduct modern high-tempo offensives.

## 5. Airpower and target-system pressure

E6 Twin and A6M/E6 Single superiority does not mean zero risk:

- Chinese AA, machine guns, weather, terrain, navigation, decoys and accidents remain;
- Chinese fighters increasingly avoid unfavorable interception rather than disappear physically.

The V24/V25 air system raises both:

1. number/quality of attack points through repeated reconnaissance and change detection;
2. efficiency of conventional bomb mass by assigning it to repaired/moved/reconstituted targets.

This can produce a paradox:

- city-center bombing may be politically restricted for US diplomacy;
- some air commanders welcome that restriction because it frees sorties/tonnage for bridges, repair yards, airfields, fuel, trucks, factories, river handling and road nodes;
- other commanders still believe city pressure is necessary to damage morale/administration;
- crews can dislike precise difficult targets while elite crews welcome observable military effects.

## 7. Burma Road / internal transport interpretation

Physical route existence is not the same as effective military delivery.

Use:
- port/rail/road handling;
- truck availability;
- fuel/spares;
- cycle time;
- transshipment;
- repair;
- inventory;
- weather;
- bombing/dispersal

as separate constraints.

V24 retained conditional Kunming processing-friction bands:
- about 8–12% in ordinary sustained pressure;
- about 12–18% in heavy-raid weeks.

These are not national GDP losses and are not added mechanically to other percentage penalties.

## 8. Fabi / fiscal state

China already begins 1941 in severe inflationary finance.

For V25 simulation purposes:

- legal/nominal government finance relies heavily on monetary issue;
- real burden shifts toward fixed-income urban groups and in-kind extraction from rural/provincial economies;
- military survival can continue even while currency quality deteriorates.

Historical-order circulation trajectory and the stronger V25 repair/logistics burden support a **WORKING mid-August band of roughly 115–125 億 fabi, center ~120 億** for simulation bookkeeping. This is a model coordinate, not a newly discovered archival exact count.

Shanghai exchange-rate movement in late July/early August is a warning indicator of confidence, not a direct measure of Sichuan consumer prices.

## 9. Political consequence

The same data support opposite conclusions:

- Chongqing can say: the front survives, US pressure has already forced Japan to pause southward expansion, and external aid may grow; therefore do not concede now.
- Chongqing fiscal/logistics officials can say: each additional month is paid for with currency quality, transport capital, technical equipment and administrative legitimacy.
- Tokyo hard-liners can say: China is finally losing the expensive layer of modern war and should not be released from pressure.
- Tokyo pessimists can say: China can convert itself into a low-capital light-infantry war and continue indefinitely unless the political problem is solved.

Keep all four interpretations live until the actors receive more evidence.
