- only selected screen ships where the geometry opens a later carrier attack corridor.

### Phase B — maneuver forcing

### Phase C — exploitation

- FT-IR/SAP;
- remaining D3A;
- later Type91 axes
exploit the changed target motion and degraded local AA picture.

Phases overlap in minutes.
They are not a scripted modern missile salvo.

## 9. Why the gain is asymmetric

The AA-suppression gain helps Type91 more than FT because:
- Type91 mothers must remain on a low/straight inner approach longer;
- FT mothers can release farther out once setup is achieved.

The gain helps D3A modestly because:
- dive bombers still expose themselves during pull-out / egress;
- director loss reduces the quality of heavy-AA tracking through the dive.

FT itself gains mainly through:
- cleaner setup;
- reduced need for last-second evasive correction by the mother aircraft;
not because the weapon becomes immune after release.
