## 3. Why A6M can absorb the role diversity

Worldline A6M cannot match Gaifu's best climb/speed envelope.

But it can cover multiple defensive jobs because:
- mature endurance permits altitude pre-positioning;
- improved radio/formation reliability improves section-level control;
- improved reacceleration helps repeated local engagements;
- common performance assumptions reduce controller error;
- common deck/fuel/maintenance cycle improves relief frequency.

Thus role diversity is produced by:
- altitude;
- fuel state;
- pilot assignment;
- readiness state;
not by aircraft type.
