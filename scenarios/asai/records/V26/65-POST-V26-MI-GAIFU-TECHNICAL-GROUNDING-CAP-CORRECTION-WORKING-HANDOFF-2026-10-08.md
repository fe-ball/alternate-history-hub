## 4. What makes Gaifu genuinely different

The system value comes from a bundle:

- modest but real higher-speed envelope;
- better climb potential;
- different powerplant/propeller response regime;
- high-lift wing architecture;
- carrier-qualified low-speed/approach design;
- different gun package / firing characteristics;
- different acceleration / energy / handling envelope to the extent supported by trials and pilot familiarity;
- separate pilot training / tactical experience.

Not every component is automatically superior in every fight.

## 5. Revised CAP-role interpretation

### Outer/high interception
Gaifu remains useful here because:
- modest speed advantage compounds with earlier warning;
- climb advantage can improve time-to-altitude;
- heavier 20-mm-centered fire may improve a short firing opportunity.

But:
- it is not a pure high-speed interceptor;
- it should not automatically outrun every F4F/SBD tactical situation;
- vectoring quality remains decisive.

### Middle CAP
Gaifu is also plausible here:
- not restricted to a high-altitude niche;
- can engage and then reposition differently from A6M.

### Inner/low CAP
A6M remains naturally important due:
- quantity;
- endurance;
- pilot familiarity;
- maneuver doctrine;
- established deck/support system.

Gaifu can participate, but assigning all Gaifu to the inner layer wastes some of its reach/climb differentiation.
