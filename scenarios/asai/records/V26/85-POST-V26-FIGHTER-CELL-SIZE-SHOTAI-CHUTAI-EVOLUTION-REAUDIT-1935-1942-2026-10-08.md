## 1. Separate three different meanings of "cell"

Do not treat one number as all of organization.

### Administrative cell
- formal personnel / aircraft accounting;
- leader billets;
- markings;
- training establishment.

### Immediate combat element
- aircraft that maneuver / attack together at the point of contact.

### Controlled tactical package
- the number of aircraft one leader can meaningfully task, split, recall and reform before/around contact.

Historically these can differ.

The communications divergence most directly raises the ceiling of the **controlled tactical package**.

## 2. Why three aircraft was sticky

The three-plane shotai fits a leader-centered visual system well:

- one shotaicho;
- two wingmen;
- compact visual control;
- easy leader-following;
- compatible with aggressive leader-centered attack;
- simple accounting inside nine-plane chutai.

It is not necessary to claim that poor radio was the only cause.

Other supports include:
- training tradition;
- leadership hierarchy;
- visual lookout / mutual observation;
- carrier establishment arithmetic;
- pilot-skill assumptions;
- maneuver doctrine centered on the strongest leader.

Therefore better radio does not automatically abolish three.

## 3. What better radio changes first

Reliable leader-level voice weakens one major constraint:

**a formation no longer has to remain physically compact merely to preserve command.**

This allows:
- greater spacing;
- detach/rejoin;
- altitude split;
- temporary loss of visual contact without automatic mission loss;
- one shotai to investigate while another holds;
- leader control of two shotai as distinct subelements.

Therefore the earliest plausible worldline change is NOT necessarily:
3 -> 4 aircraft.

It is:
**3-aircraft immediate element retained, while 6-aircraft two-shotai tactical control becomes routine.**

## 5. 1938

Continuous China combat plus more reliable radio allows two shotai to separate and rejoin with less risk.

Working development:
- three-plane shotai remains immediate element;
- six-plane **two-shotai package** emerges locally for escort / high-cover / sweep tasks;
- one senior leader can assign:
  - attacking shotai;
  - covering shotai;
and exchange roles.

This is not yet a new formal table of organization.

Drivers:

Working state:

- 9-aircraft chutai can operate as:
  - 6-aircraft main package + 3-aircraft reserve/free/high element,
rather than one rigid nine-aircraft mass.

This is an important worldline divergence without changing formal organization.

## 7. 1940 — A6M / higher-performance fighter generation

The A6M-era aircraft changes the economics of close visual formation:

- higher speed creates larger separation rates;
- long range increases the cost of losing an element;
- long escort missions reward fuel/status reporting;
- high/low cover separation becomes tactically more useful;
- a leader can no longer physically watch every aircraft continuously.

With the worldline radio culture already established:
- six-plane two-shotai maneuver packages become increasingly natural;
- nine-aircraft chutai may be controlled as 6+3;
- multi-chutai missions can be assembled from 6-aircraft packages.

This is the likely first "cell increase".

## 8. Four-aircraft cell question

A four-aircraft formation has a different requirement.

It is not just "one more aircraft under the leader."

Its strongest form is:
- two mutually supporting pairs;
- paired lookout;
- pair-level attack / cover interchange;
- more symmetric support than leader + two wingmen.

To gain the full benefit, the service must accept a change in:
- pair leadership;
- mutual-support responsibility;
- tactical geometry;
- training syllabus;
- replacement pilot expectations.

Better radio makes this easier.
It does not by itself produce the pair concept.

Therefore distinguish:

### 4-aircraft experimental use
Plausible earlier.

Selected high-speed / interceptor / elite fighter units could test:
- 2+2;
- leader pair + supporting pair;
- four-plane loose formation.

A worldline experimental window in **1940H2-1941** is plausible.

### 4-aircraft fleet standard
Not automatically closed by spring 1942.

It requires explicit doctrinal adoption and training propagation.

Historical 204 Kokutai's 1943 four-plane adoption shows the organizational concept was reachable, but not inevitable in 1941-42.

## 9. Why the worldline may discover four earlier

Compared with history:

- radios are more useful earlier;
- China combat is continuous;
- A6M long-range operations force split/rejoin problems;
- E6-family high-speed/high-cover work provides another tactical laboratory;
- fighter leaders can compare 3-plane and larger packages repeatedly.

Thus four-aircraft trials should not be deferred automatically to 1943.

Working:
- **1940H2-1941 selected-unit experiments plausible**;
- **1941H2-1942H1 limited doctrine trial plausible**;
- universal carrier adoption by Apr29 remains OPEN / not central.

## 10. Why three may still survive on carriers

Carrier aviation has extra inertia:

- established leader billets / markings;
- 18/21-class fighter establishments built around multiples of three;
- deck-cycle plans;
- mixed escort/CAP mission packages;
- rapid reassignment of pilots between carriers;
- need for common procedures across divisions;
- elite leader-centered culture.

A carrier force values interoperability more than an isolated land-based experiment.

Therefore the central spring-1942 carrier solution can be:

**formal 3-plane shotai + routine 6-plane controlled package + occasional 3-plane free/reserve element.**

This is more evolved than historical-static doctrine without requiring a premature full 4-plane conversion.

## 13. What would trigger formal four-aircraft adoption

A full 4-plane cell becomes more likely when three conditions align:

1. **communications**
   - reliable enough for loose subelements.

2. **enemy tactical pressure**
   - repeated evidence that pair mutual support defeats leader-centered three-plane attacks.

3. **training/institutional acceptance**
   - pair leaders and wingmen are trained for symmetric mutual support rather than simply protecting the ace/leader.

Worldline communications closes much of condition 1 early.

Conditions 2-3 may close earlier than history if:
- China opposition presents enough suitable encounters;
- U.S./British early-1942 combat provides strong after-action evidence.

Thus Apr29/MI can itself become an adoption accelerator.
