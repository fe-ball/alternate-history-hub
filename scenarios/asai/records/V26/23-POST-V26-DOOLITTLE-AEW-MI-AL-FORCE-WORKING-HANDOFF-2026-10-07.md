## Discussion progress

### Doolittle-type raid

Current working branch keeps Enterprise + Hornet and an Apr21-23-class raid, center Apr22.

The Japanese defense review now distinguishes:
- improved observer posts, direct phone/radio reporting, plotting rooms and readiness states;
- better local Army-internal and Navy-internal information handoff;
- but no nationwide RAF-style precision GCI and no seamless Army-Navy common air picture.

The key lesson is that interceptor top speed is not the main bottleneck once adequate fighters exist. Detection, track continuity, handoff, vectoring and readiness dominate whether an intercept actually occurs.

Current combat discussion keeps the raid broadly successful while allowing limited Japanese fighter contact and a small number of losses/damaged aircraft. Yokosuka is the strongest candidate for a successful interception because warning and base defense can remain within the Navy chain. Exact aircraft-by-aircraft results remain OPEN.
