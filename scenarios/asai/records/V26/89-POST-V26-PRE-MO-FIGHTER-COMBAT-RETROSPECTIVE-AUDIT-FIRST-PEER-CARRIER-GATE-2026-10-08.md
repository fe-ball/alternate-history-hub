## 1. What "symmetric fighter battle" means here

Air-to-air combat obviously exists before Dec 7, including China combat and Pearl local interception.

The useful distinction is:

### Tactical fighter combat
fighter versus fighter / bomber at the local engagement level.

### Peer carrier-air-system combat
both sides possess:
- mobile flight decks;
- search/contact uncertainty;
- CAP;
- fighter escort;
- launch/recovery constraints;
- reserve allocation;
- air-group command;
- mutual ability to damage the other's flight deck.

On that definition:
**the Dec 7 Enterprise engagement is the first clear peer carrier-air-system battle in the current Pacific branch.**

It is the first event where:
- Japanese escort allocation;
- U.S. radar/CAP;
- Japanese airborne strike command;
- fighter package discipline;
- reserve for an unlocated second carrier;
- valid-release survival
all interact directly.
