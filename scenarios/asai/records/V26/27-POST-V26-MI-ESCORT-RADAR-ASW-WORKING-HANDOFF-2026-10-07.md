## 9. ASW allocation summary

Main carrier cells each use the same doctrine template:
1. preserve one radar/visual picket if possible;
2. detach one or two acoustic-ASW destroyers on a plausible submerged contact;
3. slow enough for useful Type93-line reacquisition;
4. prosecute with Type95 depth charges;
5. restore the screen as soon as tactically acceptable.

Cell priorities:
- Cell 1: Nowaki / Hagikaze;
- Cell 2: Isokaze / Hamakaze;
- Cell 3 inner: Yugumo / Makigumo;

- Akigumo;
- Shiokaze.

These are operational priorities, not ship-type reclassifications.
