## 6. Apr29 carrier fighter-control package

### Ship / cell level

One Type21-line set central provides:
- coarse raid bearing;
- rough range/timing;
- no reliable precise altitude;
- no continuous fighter track.

Add:
- visual reports from Shokaku/Zuikaku/screens;
- local air plot;
- deck readiness record;
- last CAP report / expected fuel state.

Output to CAP leader:
- bearing sector;
- estimated arrival;
- rough altitude class when available;
- threat priority;
- launch / reinforce / hold order.

### CAP leader level

The senior airborne fighter leader can:
- assign shotai by sector/altitude;
- send one shotai to investigate while retaining another;
- order engage / hold-high / cover-low / rejoin / return;
- request/expect relief.

He cannot:
- see a complete plot;
- know every shotai's exact position/fuel;
- receive continuous precision vectors.

### Shotai level

Three aircraft remain the normal immediate combat element.

Radio can support:
- contact call;
- break;
- attack order;
- cover;
- rejoin;
- return.

Once combat fragments:
- visual judgment and pilot initiative dominate;
- radio traffic, workload and range reduce control quality.

## 8. Communication gain in the defensive sequence

Compared with historical-poor-radio execution, the gain is:

1. contact report reaches the CAP leader more reliably;
2. CAP leader can leave a shotai high while sending another down;
3. a detached shotai can be recalled/rejoined;
4. relief aircraft can be sent toward the correct broad sector;
5. duplicated pursuit is somewhat reduced;
6. low-fuel fighters can report/withdraw without silently disappearing from the controller's expectations.

The gain is strongest before and between engagements.

It is weakest inside a close dogfight.

## 9. Communication gain in the escort sequence

A15's five shotai need not fly as one indivisible fighter mass.

Possible tasking:
- high/free cover;
- D3A escort;
- B5N escort;
- reserve / cross-support.

Useful radio calls:
- enemy fighter contact;
- which bomber group is under attack;
- remain with bombers;
- do not chase;
- rejoin at bearing/point;
- disengage / return.

This can improve attack-chain completion without increasing fighter gunnery lethality.
