## 3. Air-launched ramjet flying-torpedo line — working decisions

### 3.1 Carrier/interface

Primary design requirement:
- fit the next carrier attack aircraft / B5N class;
- Type 91-like mass/length/diameter / CG / handling ecology where practical;
- dedicated cradle/ejector is allowed;
- E5 Twin is a high-speed development tool, not the required operational carrier.

Initial article targets the Type 91 Mod 1 mass class rather than immediately using the heavier Mod 2 class.

### 3.2 Wing/carriage

Working:
- fixed straight wing is acceptable;
- no mandatory folding/deploying wing for the first article;
- near-zero weapon-wing incidence during normal carriage;
- carrier takeoff angle of attack may allow the weapon wing to carry part of its apparent weight;
- the objective is to avoid making B5N takeoff materially worse than the ordinary 800 kg torpedo case, not to claim a major free takeoff-performance gain;
- X/oblique geometry remains a later packaging option.

### 3.3 Positive separation

Working spring-ejector concept:
- two synchronized points;
- ~0.10–0.15 m stroke;
- ~5–10 kN total average downward force;
- ~0.5–1.5 kJ stored energy;
- roughly ~20–40 kg complete cradle/ejector increment.

Purpose is reliable separation only. It is not a forward-speed booster.

### 3.4 RATO posture

Baseline: **NO RATO**.

Instead:
- carrier makes a shallow descent and builds launch speed;
- attached low-flow pre-light / pilot-flame confirmation may be used;
- full ramjet thrust remains inhibited until separation unless later testing proves attached full-light safe;
- after separation the weapon descends ~15–20 degrees, establishes useful ramjet thrust, then makes a gentle pull-out.

Working B5N-class initial-test band:
- release speed ~350–380 km/h class;
- release altitude ~600–1,000 m central;
- higher altitude / RATO can be reopened only if the ramjet useful-thrust speed proves too high.

### 3.5 Control

Early control is a simple state machine, not modern guidance:
- standby;
- launch condition;
- separation;
- ignition/descent;
- dynamic-pressure / thrust gate;
- pull-out;
- low-height capture;
- terminal run.

Gyro, pitot/dynamic-pressure switch, timer/cam, simple servos and combustion confirmation are sufficient candidates at this stage.

### 3.6 Height sensor

**WORKING DECISION: RADIO ALTIMETER.**

Barometric altitude may remain a coarse backup/transition reference.

Exact radio-altimeter design, frequency, antennas, sea-return behavior, reliable minimum height and development clock remain OPEN.

### 3.7 Warhead-height doctrine

Two families remain:
- HE / blast-fragmentation;
- semi-AP / APHE-like.

Working decision:
- the early semi-AP/APHE-type is **not** the routine anti-destroyer configuration;
- a terminal height optimized for larger-ship side/upper-hull impact may pass over smaller destroyers and that trade is acceptable;
- HE may use a somewhat higher path and has separate future scope for fragmentation/directivity work.

Exact terminal heights are NOT closed.
