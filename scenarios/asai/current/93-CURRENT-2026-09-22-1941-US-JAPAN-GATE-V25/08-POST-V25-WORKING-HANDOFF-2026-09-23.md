## 3. Synthetic-fuel / hydrogen reconstruction — conversation working close

### Asai hydrogen role

- Asai is **not** the prime national coal-liquefaction producer.
- Asai's role is subsystem / enabling-industry supply:
  - alkaline water electrolysis and high-purity H2 where appropriate;
  - gas purification/drying;
  - multistage reciprocating bulk compression;
  - selected metal-diaphragm high-purity compression;
  - buffer/storage;
  - high-pressure piping;
  - valves/seals;
  - pressure vessels;
  - metallurgy and hydrogen degradation / embrittlement work;
  - instrumentation/safety;
  - NDT, QC, failure analysis and maintenance.
- Large bulk hydrogen for direct coal hydrogenation should mainly come from coal/water-gas/coke-oven routes rather than assuming universal electrolysis.
- Asai is also a major **fuel-quality demand-side user** through GT/diesel/vehicles/generators and therefore supplies useful fuel-property feedback.
- The previously cited parent `asai-works-gt-fuel.md` was not recovered in the current tree; do not pretend the missing original was found.

### Synthetic-fuel technical baseline before the later German transfer

- Low-temperature carbonization / tar / shale: technically mature or near-mature by the late 1930s, with economic/byproduct constraints.
- FT: commercial introduction already underway by 1940. The first German connection is the earlier normal Ruhrchemie/Koppers commercial transfer/license, before the later state E5 counter-transfer.
- Direct coal hydrogenation: scientific/pilot/basic process known, large equipment and partial runs exist, but stable reproducible commercial continuous operation remains weak; do not credit large strategic output before the later transfer.
- Tar hydrogenation/refining/upgrading: known and Asai-adjacent.
- Shale oil: separate practical route and should not be counted as direct coal-liquefaction progress.

### German transfer distinction

Two lines must remain separate:

1. pre-existing commercial FT transfer/license;
2. autumn 1940 state-to-state E5 counter-transfer with high-pressure chemistry / synthetic fuel / rubber / catalysts / machine-tool know-how.

The second line transfers high-density engineering knowledge: flowsheets/specifications, reactor materials/design/inspection, pressure piping/joints/valves, compressor/pump failure data, gas cleanup/recycle H2, startup/shutdown/purge procedures, operating records, catalyst preparation/poisoning/life where available, feed prep/product separation/upgrading, maintenance/failure taxonomy and advisers.
