## 2. Soviet preference in August 1941

USSR strategic preference is two-sided:

- keep Japan out of the Soviet war;
- keep substantial Japanese power tied down in China.

A China settlement that releases Japanese divisions without a force-disposition assurance can therefore look dangerous to Moscow even if it ends Sino-Japanese fighting.

Soviet material aid to China is constrained by the German war, but political signaling can still raise Chongqing's reservation value.
