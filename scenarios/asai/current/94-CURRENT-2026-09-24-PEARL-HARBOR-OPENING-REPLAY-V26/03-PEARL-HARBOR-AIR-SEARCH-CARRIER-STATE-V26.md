# V26 PEARL HARBOR / SEARCH / CARRIER STATE

## 1. Oahu attack conversion model

Do not model worldline advantage as one generic hit-rate bonus.

Required chain:

target-area arrival -> formation/communication integrity -> attack setup -> valid release -> weapon function -> geometry/hit -> damage -> BDA -> follow-on decision.

A6M, B5N and D3A gains occur mostly in the early/middle links: serviceability, radio, formation, instrument/release repeatability, escort coordination and turnaround.

## 2. Communication interpretation

The worldline has a long aircraft-radio/EMI lineage beginning in the early 1930s:

- ignition shielding;
- bonding;
- generator suppression;
- power filtering;
- better connectors and acceptance tests;
- institutional familiarity with radio flash reports.

This does **not** create modern datalink or perfect fighter direction.

For Pearl replay it supports:

- more reliable leader-to-leader short coded transmissions;
- less dependence on one ambiguous visual flare;
- better section/chutai deconfliction;
- faster flash BDA;
- more usable H7Y/E13A position/course updates.

## 3. Carrier search architecture

Local layer:
- Tone/Chikuma E13A for Pearl/Lahaina confirmation.

Close screen:
- cruiser/battleship floatplanes for local warning and fleet screening.

Fixed/barrier layer:
- historical Hawaii submarine deployment.

Outer ocean layer:
- H7Y-D1: Oahu-west carrier-return corridor;
- H7Y-D2: Hawaii–Midway / likely second-carrier corridor.

The point of H7Y is not raw range alone. It fills the wide-area ocean-search gap without consuming the B5N/D3A carrier-strike pool.

## 4. Contact semantics

Use three levels:

- CONTACT-1: fleet found; carrier presence / rough position.
- CONTACT-2: classification + course + speed good enough for planning.
- CONTACT-3-like: repeated updates sufficient to launch toward a moving intercept point.

Central branch:

- Enterprise reaches CONTACT-2 around 08:17–08:20.
- H7Y/E13A relay sustains a usable CONTACT-3-like track long enough for the noon strike.
- Lexington remains unlocated.

## 5. Enterprise god-view vs Japanese knowledge

God-view:
- Enterprise is Oahu-west and can be attacked the same day.
- Lexington is much farther toward the Midway-southeast side and is not an immediate same-day strike threat.

Japanese knowledge:
- Enterprise is known and tracked.
- Lexington is missing.
- Therefore Kido Butai must retain a non-trivial CAP/reserve rather than committing every fighter to Enterprise.

US knowledge:
- Enterprise knows Pearl has been attacked.
- Enterprise suspects Japanese reconnaissance has found it.
- Enterprise still lacks a reliable Kido Butai position before the Japanese carrier strike.

## 6. Third-strike aircraft state

Central package: 72 aircraft.

This is not an "extra" inventory layer.

It is made by:

- recovering first-wave aircraft;
- inspecting and selecting serviceable aircraft;
- pre-staging anti-ship torpedoes / SAP bombs from 08:20 onward;
- dividing carrier deck responsibilities so not all six carriers must finish every recovery task before strike spotting begins.

Fifth Carrier Division remains especially useful for CAP / late recovery / reserve burden while First and Second Carrier Divisions form the main anti-Enterprise strike.

## 7. Enterprise damage central

At 14:20 central:

- 3 x 250 kg-class bomb hits;
- 2 aerial torpedo hits;
- flight operations impossible;
- hangar/fire damage;
- major flooding;
- machinery/shaft/steering damage sensitivity;
- 10–15 kt-class residual speed;
- afloat but mission-killed.

Do not promote "Enterprise sunk" at this clock.

## 8. Submarine geometry

Worldline submarine improvements:

- high-power machinery is more repeatably usable;
- dive-transition measurement is better;
- batteries/ventilation/QC are better;
- noise maps and radio are better;
- Type 95 gyro/depth/launch repeatability is better.

Not granted:

- extra knots by fiat;
- extra crush depth by fiat;
- FCS3 on every old boat;
- instant convergence on a carrier contact.

Relevant 7 Dec candidates:

- I-74: strongest west/southwest candidate;
- I-2 / I-3: Kauai-channel / northern return gate;
- I-68 / I-69 / I-75: southwest-Oahu return gate.

Selected submarines should intercept predicted future track. They do not chase a carrier from far astern.
