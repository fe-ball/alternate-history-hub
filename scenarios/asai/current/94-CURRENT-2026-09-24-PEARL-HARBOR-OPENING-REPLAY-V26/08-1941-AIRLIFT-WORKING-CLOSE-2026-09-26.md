- do not use as a short / soft newly captured strip transport;

## 10. Southern Operations throughput interpretation

Working Southern conventional-air-transport model:

- 74 routine-eligible assigned aircraft after airborne reservations;
- 58 ready center;
- model effective delivered leg-tonnage around **15.6 t/day** under the stated readiness / utilization assumptions.

Comparison case with weaker serviceability:
- 50 ready;
- ~13.4 t/day on the same mission model.

Interpretation:
- worldline advantage is a few tons/day plus lower critical-part latency, not a national bulk logistics revolution;
- multi-leg freight is not counted twice as final-delivered cargo;
- E5/E6 courier sorties reduce the need to waste larger transports on 100–300 kg urgent loads;
- P2 / Ki-76 reduce last-leg road dependence for small critical cargo;
- L5Si creates a new limited capability to move large high-value aviation loads quickly.

Do not apply a blanket "% logistics bonus" to all Southern formations.

## 11. Forward use in replay

At each captured airfield / port:

1. identify the actual missing item or personnel;
2. identify whether it fits the aircraft door / floor / CG / range;
3. allocate an actual transport sortie;
4. charge weather / runway / fuel / unloading;
5. only then change aircraft readiness / base opening time.

No automatic "airlift improves every captured base by N days."
