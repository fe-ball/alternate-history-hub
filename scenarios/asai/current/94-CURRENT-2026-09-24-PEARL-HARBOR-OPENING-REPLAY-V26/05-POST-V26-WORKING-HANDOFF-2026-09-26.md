## 1. Method guards carried forward

1. No teleology. Do not use phrases/logic equivalent to “this worldline naturally wants X” or infer later doctrine from later outcomes.
2. Actor knowledge only. Better equipment does not automatically create modern doctrine, FAC/CAS, anti-radar campaign planning, perfect target prioritization, or omniscient BDA.
3. Separate:
   - physical existence,
   - factory acceptance,
   - service release,
   - unit assignment,
   - operational readiness,
   - actual combat presence.
4. New technology must satisfy range/base/support constraints. A high-performance aircraft that cannot reach the target or lacks forward support does not participate.
5. Weather, visibility, geography, bridge demolition, road congestion and human fatigue can erase technical advantages.
6. Historical success already near a practical ceiling should not receive a second large bonus merely because worldline equipment is better.
7. When an historical event depends on a changed prior disposition, re-roll geometry rather than copying the historical result automatically.
